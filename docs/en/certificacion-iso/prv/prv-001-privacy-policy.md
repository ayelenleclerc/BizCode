## Out-of-scope statement

This document describes BizCode product privacy controls for customer personal data. It does not claim ISO/IEC 27701 certification.


## Purpose

Define how personal data of customers (`Cliente`) is inventoried, accessed, rectified, and anonymized in BizCode, and point operators to AAIP registration duties.
